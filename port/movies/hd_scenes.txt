# The theater's HD scenes the port plays in place of the game's own event scenes: the
# videos of the HD collection's 358/2 Days theater, made from the player's own copy into
# data/cutscenes/cinematics/<stem>.mp4, played instead of the scene's skippable stretch, the game's
# own skip ending it. A mod's cutscenes/scenes.txt adds entries or replaces these by their script.
#
# <script> <video stem> [<video stem> ...] ; <evidence>
#   <script>  an event archive's entry, EV_<world>#<index> (ev/EV_S.p2's entry 4: EV_S#4), a
#             mission's script section, mi/<mission>/<script>:<section>, or a day's monologue,
#             mnl#<record> (ui/mnl's text record, picked by the story's day)
#   off       in place of the stems: the game's own scene (a mod may switch an entry off)
#   skip <video stem>  the scene skipped with no video when the movie just before it was shown as
#             that HD video, which shows the scene too (the game's own scene otherwise)
# mv#<movie> <video stem> [...] ; <evidence>
#             a game's movie shown as those videos in a row (hd<movie> alone without an entry);
#             or `off`, the game's own movie
# A scene's or a movie's video stem may be a part of it, <video stem>[<from>:<until>]: the video
# between those seconds (either left out: its own start or end).
