# The panels: the last three of the panels' table's blank records (513-630: no name, no kind, left out of the camp),
# the only ones no save owns (513-627 are items a game gives, named elsewhere). The hold grows by level like the
# series' Stop, each enemy's own resistance to freezing shortening it; the reach is the clock's round its caster (the
# game made its effect to stand round the one who casts it). Prices: above every other magic's (theirs 200, 300,
# 400 by level), Stop being strong.
